# Salary bands FY2026-27 (HR only)

Internal. Don't share outside HR and the leadership team.

All figures are annual base salary in lakhs per annum (LPA).

## Engineering

| Level | Title | Base salary |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate Engineer | ₹8–12 LPA |
| L2 | Software Engineer | ₹12–20 LPA |
| L3 | Senior Software Engineer | ₹20–35 LPA |
| L4 | Staff Engineer | ₹35–55 LPA |
| L5 | Principal Engineer | ₹55–80 LPA |

## Customer support

| Title | Base salary |
|---|---|
| Support Associate | ₹4–6 LPA |
| Senior Support Associate | ₹6–9 LPA |
| Support Lead | ₹9–14 LPA |

## Sales

| Title | Base salary | Variable pay |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Development Representative | ₹6–9 LPA | up to 25% of base |
| Account Executive | ₹12–20 LPA | 30–50% of base |

## Interns

- Engineering interns: ₹40,000 per month
- Other interns: ₹25,000 per month

## Revisions and bonus

Salaries are revised every April. Employees at L3 and above are eligible for a performance bonus of up to 15% of base salary.
