# Getting started with Acme Cloud

Acme Cloud is file storage and sharing for small teams. Your team's files live in a **workspace**, and everyone you invite to the workspace can work on them together.

## Create a workspace

1. Sign up at app.acme-cloud.example with your work email.
2. Open the verification email and click the link. The link is valid for 24 hours.
3. Choose a workspace name. Your workspace address becomes `<name>.acme-cloud.example`. You can change it once every 30 days in Settings → General.

## Invite your team

Go to Settings → Members → Invite and enter email addresses. Invitations expire after 7 days; you can resend them from the same page.

Each person has one role:

- **Owner**: one per workspace. Manages billing, can delete the workspace and can transfer ownership to an Admin.
- **Admin**: manages members, security settings and integrations. Cannot change billing.
- **Member**: uploads, edits and shares files.
- **Guest**: sees only the folders shared with them. Guests are free and don't count as paid seats. A workspace can have up to 50 guests.

## Upload files

| Where | Maximum file size | Supported versions |
|---|---|---|
| Web app | 5 GB | Latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari |
| Desktop sync app | 50 GB | Windows 10+, macOS 13+, Ubuntu 22.04+ |
| Mobile app | 5 GB | Android 10+, iOS 16+ |

The desktop app syncs one folder on your computer with your workspace. Files you delete go to **Trash**, where they stay for 30 days on Free, 90 days on Team and 180 days on Business. After that they are deleted permanently and can't be recovered.

## Share files

Right-click a file or folder and choose **Share**. You can share with people in your workspace or create a share link.

- Share links can allow **view** or **edit** access.
- On Team and Business, you can protect a link with a password and set an expiry date.
- Admins can turn off share links for the whole workspace in Settings → Security.
