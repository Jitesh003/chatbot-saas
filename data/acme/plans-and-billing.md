# Plans and billing

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Storage | Members | Version history |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | 5 GB total | Up to 3 | 30 days |
| Team | ₹499 per user per month | 1 TB per user, pooled | Unlimited | 180 days |
| Business | ₹999 per user per month | 5 TB per user, pooled | Unlimited | 1 year |

Business also includes single sign-on (SSO) and the audit log. "Pooled" means the whole team shares one storage total: a 4-person Team workspace gets 4 TB to use however it likes.

## How billing works

- You can pay **monthly** or **annually**. Annual billing costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free.
- Prices don't include GST. We add 18% GST and email a GST invoice on each billing date. Add your GSTIN in Settings → Billing to get it on the invoice.
- Payment methods: credit or debit card, UPI AutoPay, and net banking (net banking only for annual plans).
- **Adding a member** in the middle of a billing period: you pay a prorated amount for the remaining days.
- **Removing a member**: the unused amount becomes credit on your next invoice. It isn't refunded as cash.

## Changing plans

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You pay the prorated difference for the rest of the billing period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing period.
- If you downgrade to Free while using more than 5 GB, you can still view and download everything, but you can't upload until you're under 5 GB.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry on day 1, day 3 and day 5 and email the Owner each time. If the payment still hasn't gone through after **7 days**, the workspace becomes **read-only**: everyone can view and download files, but nobody can upload or edit. Paying the invoice restores full access immediately. After 30 days unpaid, the workspace moves to the Free plan.

## Cancelling

Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. Your paid features continue until the end of the period you've already paid for, then the workspace moves to Free.

## Refunds

- **Monthly plans** are not refundable.
- **Annual plans** are fully refundable within **14 days** of purchase or renewal. After 14 days, we don't refund annual plans.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
