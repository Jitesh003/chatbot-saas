# Returns, refunds and cancellations

## Return window

You can return items within **7 days of delivery**. The 7 days are counted from the delivery date shown in your order tracking.

## What can be returned

- **Unopened tea packs** with the seal intact: full refund.
- **Gift boxes**: returnable only if the outer seal is intact.
- **Kulhads** (clay cups): returnable if unused. If they arrive broken or chipped, tell us within **48 hours of delivery** and send photos. We'll send a free replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.
- **Opened tea packs** can't be returned, unless we sent the wrong item or the tea arrived damaged or spoiled. In that case, tell us within 7 days of delivery with a photo.

## How to return

1. Email help@chaiandco.example (or reply to your order confirmation) with your order number, such as CHAI-1003, and the email address you used for the order.
2. We arrange a free pickup within 2 business days.
3. After pickup, we check the items within 2 business days, then issue your refund.

You can return some items from an order and keep the rest. You're refunded only for the items you return.

## Refunds

- **Prepaid orders** (card, UPI, net banking, wallet): refunded to the original payment method. The money reaches you 5–7 business days after we issue the refund.
- **Cash on delivery (COD) orders**: refunded by UPI or bank transfer (NEFT) within 3 business days of issuing the refund. We'll ask for your UPI ID or bank details.
- Shipping is free, so nothing is deducted for shipping. The ₹30 COD fee is not refunded.

## Cancelling an order

- **Before it's dispatched**: cancel from your order page or email us. You get a full refund.
- **After it's dispatched**: you can't cancel. You can refuse the delivery (we refund you once the package comes back to us, usually within 7 days) or request a return after it's delivered.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. Return the item and place a new order.
