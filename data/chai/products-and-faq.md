## How do I make masala chai?

For one cup: boil half a cup of water with 1 teaspoon of Masala Chai for 1 minute, add half a cup of milk, simmer for 3–4 minutes, add sugar to taste, and strain.

## How much caffeine is in a cup?

About 40–50 mg per cup for our CTC blends, which is roughly half a cup of coffee.

## How long does the tea stay fresh?

12 months from the packing date printed on the pack. Once opened, keep it in an airtight container away from moisture and strong smells.
