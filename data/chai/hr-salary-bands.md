# Salary bands 2026 (HR only)

Internal. Don't share outside HR and the founders.

All figures are annual base salary in lakhs per annum (LPA).

## Operations

| Title | Base salary |
|---|---|
| Warehouse Associate | ₹2.4–3.2 LPA |
| Warehouse Supervisor | ₹4–5.5 LPA |
| Operations Manager | ₹10–15 LPA |

## Customer support

| Title | Base salary |
|---|---|
| Customer Support Executive | ₹3–4.2 LPA |
| Support Team Lead | ₹5–7 LPA |

## Marketing

| Title | Base salary |
|---|---|
| Performance Marketing Executive | ₹5–8 LPA |
| Marketing Manager | ₹12–18 LPA |

## Interns

₹15,000 per month for all interns.

## Bonus

Staff with at least 6 months of service get a Diwali bonus equal to one month's base salary.
