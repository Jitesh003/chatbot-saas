# Shipping policy

## Where we ship

We ship everywhere in India except the Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep. We don't ship internationally.

## Shipping cost

Shipping is **free** on every order.

## Dispatch

- Orders placed **before 2 PM IST, Monday to Saturday**, are dispatched the same day.
- Orders placed after 2 PM, or on a Sunday or national holiday, are dispatched the next working day.

## Delivery times (after dispatch)

| Destination | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune) | 2–4 business days |
| Other cities and towns | 4–7 business days |
| North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh | 7–10 business days |

## Tracking your order

When your order is dispatched, we send a tracking number (it looks like `TRK-100002`) by SMS and email. Track it at chaiandco.example/track.

## Cash on delivery (COD)

- COD is available for orders up to **₹2,000**.
- There's a **₹30 COD fee**.
- Orders above ₹2,000 must be paid online.

## Changing your address

You can change the delivery address only **before** the order is dispatched. Email help@chaiandco.example with your order number.

## Failed deliveries

The courier tries to deliver 3 times. If all 3 attempts fail, the package comes back to us and we refund prepaid orders in full.

## Late or lost orders

If your order hasn't arrived **10 business days after dispatch**, email us. We'll check with the courier and either send the order again for free or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer.
